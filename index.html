To Me

Hey, I’m Andre. With me you’ll find a Sydney based creator who loves all mediums of creative expression, though I do have my favourites. A 3D artist first, with a focus on rigging, modelling and UV unwrapping, I’m also proficient in game design, graphic design, a few coding languages and performance. A jack of all trades, and a master of some. As a designer, I’m always looking to learn new skills and ways to approach problems. I strive to reach a flow state in which I know exactly what I need to do to best achieve my goal. My problem solving skills and ability to adapt are my biggest strengths, allowing me to understand new concepts rapidly.
